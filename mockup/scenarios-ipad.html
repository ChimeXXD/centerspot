Scenarios on the Formation sheet

A ready-made start: pick your shape, then a situation (a team shape, a small-sided game or a set piece), say who has the ball, and the board is set up with both teams. Every picture below is built by mockup/scenarios.js from presets/formations.json, the same rules the app will run, so what you see is what you get.

Frames: Shapes tab · Scenarios tab, nothing picked · 3v2 counter picked · the same, defending · 7-a-side · replacing players on a board · the board after. The iPad sheet is in scenarios-ipad.html.
