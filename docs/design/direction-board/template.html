CenterSpot: three directions

The same three screens in three looks: the board with the 4-3-3, the demo move playing at step 4 (a run, a pass and a cross), and the export sheet with the 9:16 video. Layout and content are the same in each; only the look changes. Pick the one that feels like CenterSpot. Positions come from examples/overlap-left.tactic.

Fixed in every direction: home players are filled discs, away players are rings (D-15); runs are solid, passes dashed, dribbles wavy; the export carries a small "CenterSpot" mark (D-38); no club badges or real kits (D-13).

Direction board for Day 1 (D-12, D-22). Fonts from Google Fonts stand in for the app's final type, which is chosen with the direction.
