## Pattern summary

**What the picks share**
- **Vertical pitch, tokens as discs with the number inside and the name underneath** (1–7). The broadcast versions (theScore, DAZN) read best: dark pitch, white lines at low opacity, bright tokens. A formation tag ("4-3-3") sits in a corner.
- **Editors put the picture on top and the timeline below**, with a playhead, **+** at the end of the strip, undo/redo next to Play, and one bright **Export** button top right (9–14).
- **Steps are chapters.** A segmented progress bar, one segment per chapter (Brink), is the cleanest way to show "step 3 of 5" while playing (27–31).
- **Honest export choices**: segmented options with one plain sentence each, and the file size printed beside the choice (15–20). Aspect ratios work best drawn as frame-shaped tiles (Grok).
- **Drawing tools float** in a pill at the bottom and leave the canvas clear; a ✓ ends drawing mode (21–26).
- **Libraries are grids of tall thumbnails** with one strong "New" action (32–36).

**What to avoid**
- Player photos, kit shirts and club badges (8, 2–7 use real clubs): D-13.
- Bright flat green with thin white lines: numbers lose contrast and hairlines vanish after video compression (UI-BRIEF §1.2).
- Event badges or ratings on top of numbers; tab bars and extra chrome around the pitch.
- Colour as the only difference between teams (several lineups only switch colour): D-15.

**Open questions for the direction board**
- Dark broadcast, chalkboard or light print: which reads best in a 9:16 clip on WhatsApp and Discord?
- Token size: SPEC §4's 4.2% of pitch width is about 15 pt on an iPhone, which looks too small next to every reference (they sit at 6–8%). The directions use about 6.5%.
- Steps strip: numbered chips, or Brink-style segments that become the scrubber when playing?
