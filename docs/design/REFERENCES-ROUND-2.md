Screen links stay local in `reference-board/images/links.md` (git-ignored: other apps' UI is copyrighted, D-12). Never name the reference library in the repo.

Many sports screens show real clubs, kits, crests or player photos. They are here for structure only (D-13). "[untitled]" means the app's name was not available.

## Patterns by surface

Written for the dark Floodlight × Chalk look: near-black chrome, a dark pitch with white chalk lines, one electric lime accent.

### 1. Formation / lineup picker
- The tile shows the shape itself: thin white line diagrams on black (VSCO 47), big numerals with line labels (MLS 39), or a pitch of placeholder slots (Premier League 37).
- The selected tile changes as a whole, either a solid lime fill with dark text (Peloton 43) or a bright outline while the others dim (VSCO 47). Use it for the selected formation.
- Size segments (11 / 7 / 5-a-side) sit above the grid, and one capsule is pinned at the bottom (theScore 45, Peloton 43).
- Avoid: kits, crests and photo thumbnails (D-13); bright flat green mini pitches that fight the dark board.

### 2. Board editor
- A selection is thin bounds plus a small floating action pill next to the object, never a full toolbar swap (Apple Mail 53, Freeform 55).
- Undo, redo and zoom share one pill or an edge rail, so the pitch stays clear (Craft 56, Obsidian 59).
- theScore's shot map (50) is the render target: chalk on near-black with one bright path. Handwritten labels and curved hand-drawn arrows (Craft 62) suit the notes layer.
- Avoid: dot-grid backgrounds and stacked toolbars; menus or badges that cover the selected token's number.

### 3. Player / token inspector
- Dark sheets list options as title, one-line description and check (Claude 75, Luma 77). Name and Number share one row (Tempo 72); the number uses a number pad (Withings 70).
- Roles are grouped by line with counts (FocusFlight 79, MLS 66); Delete sits apart at the bottom (Photoroom 68).
- While a token is edited, everything else on the pitch dims (Premier League 67).
- Avoid: long light-mode forms; a 0–99 wheel or scrolling list for the number.

### 4. Steps strip
- Numbered chips with the active one ringed (CapCut 83), a duration under each (Snapchat 94), and **+** at the end or pinned to the current item (Tinder 86, Unfold 88).
- Duplicate · Delete appear only while a step is selected, as two or three verbs (Edits 90, Instagram 92).
- Reorder is long-press lift onto a darker slab (How We Feel 99), with one-time hint copy ("Tap to edit or long press to reorder"). A numbered list sheet handles long reorders (Canva 97).
- Avoid: action bars with six or more verbs (CapCut 89); thumbnails as the only label, since tactic frames look alike. Keep the step number visible.

### 5. Playback
- The scrubber is split into one segment per step (Ahead 105) or ticked at each step (SiriusXM 102). The current step's name sits near it (Queue 103, YouTube 104, Lovi 107 "Step 2 of 6").
- Speed is a few discrete values (0.5× / 1× / 2×), shown as segments or a short rail with the current value white or lime (TikTok 111, Facebook 101). Loop is one toggle beside speed (Fabric 109).
- Lime marks only the live element: the current step, the playing state (pliability 116).
- Avoid: ±10 s skip buttons (step back and forward should jump between steps); free sliders with 10 speeds.

### 6. Drawing
- A floating two-tier bar: tools on the lower row, the selected tool's options on the upper row (Craft 120, Fabric 127).
- Line style is a row of icons that draw the line itself, with the selected cell filled: solid, dashed, wavy for run, pass, dribble (Freeform 147, Craft 131, Docusign 135 for the squiggle).
- Arrow ends get their own small menu, and a curved run gets a midpoint handle (Freeform 145, 146, 148).
- Draw mode has an explicit exit: ✕ / title / ✓, or a Done capsule (X 136, Telegram 139, Cursor 137).
- Avoid: pen-tip tray imagery (it reads as notes, not tactics); full rainbow swatch grids. We need the kit colours and lime.

### 7. Export
- Each choice is segmented with one plain sentence and an estimated size ("Current scene: 34MB", Riveo 150; "Estimated file size: 36.2 MB", CapCut 151; "can be 6 times larger", WhatsApp 159). This is rule 7.
- Ratios are frame-shaped tiles labelled with words plus numbers ("Story (9:16)", ChatGPT 176; Whering 179).
- Progress is shown on the 9:16 preview with an honest count ("899/900 frames rendered - 0s remaining", 160) and a keep-the-app-open note (Riveo 166).
- The done state shows the preview, says where the file went ("saved to your camera roll", Edits 167) and leads straight to Share.
- Avoid: spinners with no number; the size hidden on a separate settings page.

### 8. Share
- One sheet: the preview card on top, a row of round icons with Copy link first, then one strong primary (NYTimes 194, Any Distance 180). A full-width lime primary on black works (yope 198).
- Copy is confirmed in place, with the icon turning to "✓ Copied" (Suno 201), or with a short toast. No alert.
- QR and link are equal alternatives ("Scan me / or / Send as a link", Slopes 192). The QR sits on a white tile even on a dark sheet so it scans (Crypto.com 189).
- The file name and size show in the share header (PlayStation 183).
- Avoid: platform-branded gradient buttons as the primary; a QR code with no short link or explainer next to it.

### 9. Library / launch
- "New" comes first and has a different shape from the projects: an outlined or dashed **+** tile (Play 215, Universe 205).
- Cards are tall, with a name, one line of format ("11-a-side · 5 steps", after Canva 204) and a "…" menu (Unfold 203).
- A starting-point sheet with three tiles (Unfold 214) maps to Formation / Example / Blank pitch.
- The system document browser (Freeform 209, Kino 208) is what DocumentGroup gives us; theme around it, don't rebuild it.
- Avoid: upsell banners over the grid; extra library tabs that duplicate Recents and Browse.

### 10. Onboarding, empty and error states
- Hand-drawn arrows, underlines and circles that point at real UI (GoPro Quik 222 and 227, Rodeo 239, Swiggy 241) carry the handwritten-note style straight into first run.
- Tips sit next to the tool palette with a counter ("2/3") and Skip (Freeform 238, MD Vinyl 240), not as full-screen carousels.
- Error copy names the file and the limit, then offers one way out: "must be less than 15MB" (Posh 230), reasons listed with ✕ bullets (Starling 232), the file as a chip (Alan 233), a valid range (Runna 237).
- Empty states are one line of caps or bold text, one sentence, one capsule (Kino 219, Character AI 221).
- Avoid: bare "Oops, something went wrong"; colourful illustrated empty states that break the near-black look.

### 11. Settings, sides and colours
- A swatch is a fill-and-ink pair (Apple Watch 250). Away swatches are rings (Locket 255, Any Distance 260), matching home filled / away ring on the pitch (D-15).
- A live preview sits above the swatches, the selected swatch gets a contrasting ring (Character AI 252, Revolut 258), and every colour has a name (adidas 257).
- App look and export look are separate settings (Play 247).
- Avoid: a full spectrum picker by default (keep it as a fallback, yope 256); pastels that lose contrast on charcoal.

### 12. Dark sports and broadcast UIs
- Near-black cards with one hot accent for time and state: lime minutes and a lime tab underline (DAZN 268, 271, 289); everything else white.
- Scores use giant condensed tabular numerals, and the inactive side is dimmed rather than recoloured (Apple Sports 276 and 290, NFL 292). Use the same for the current step versus the others.
- A vertical event spine with minute labels (MLS 266, theScore 267, DAZN 278) is a model for a step list with notes. The formation appears as a plain caption ("4-3-3", theScore 277, Apple Sports 276).
- Clip-title copy is short and factual ("Goal: … 27'", MLS 283). Use it for the export title band.
- Avoid: crests, kits, player photos and sponsor marks (D-13); sides that differ only by a colour bar (D-15).

### 13. Undo, toasts and confirmations
- A dark rounded toast above the bottom bar names the object ('Removed "Paris"', The Weather Channel 297) and puts **Undo** in the accent at the right (Best Buy 294). Lime on near-black.
- Undo and redo are one joined capsule, dimmed when there's no history (Universe 308, Pinterest 309).
- Destructive dialogs state the consequence in one line and word the safe option as a verb: "Back to edit" (GoPro 301), "Resume" (Ladder 306).
- Avoid: confirmation dialogs for anything undoable. Use the toast, and keep dialogs for "replace formation" and "delete tactic". Reserve red for permanent loss.

### 14. Text, captions and title overlays
- A small fixed set of styles shown as live previews (Photoroom 324, LinkedIn 325, Trip.com 317), not a font browser. A "Handwritten" category exists in real editors (Edits 316).
- Broadcast lower thirds pair a big condensed title with one small line on a solid or translucent band (Peacock 329, Sora 330). Per-step notes go on their own translucent boxes (Formula 1 332).
- Text on a solid block stays readable over any frame (Facebook 319, TikTok 314). "Apply to all" keeps step notes consistent.
- Avoid: neon or glow text effects and 40-font grids; club or league marks in title bands (D-13).
